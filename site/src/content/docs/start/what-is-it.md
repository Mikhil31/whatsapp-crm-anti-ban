This CRM is a shared inbox and a bulk-messaging tool for WhatsApp. Your team answers customers from one screen, you keep a contact book with tags, and you send the same message to many people without sending it all at once.

## Two kinds of people

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Agent** | Work in the inbox, reply, add notes, tag contacts, import contacts, make and run campaigns. |
| **Admin** | Everything an agent can, plus: manage the team, change the AI assistant, see the security page, export contacts, add extra people to a chat, and knowingly override a few safety rules (always with a written reason that is saved). |

Things only admins can do are marked with *(admin only)* in this guide.

## The one thing to understand about WhatsApp

:::caution
This product talks to WhatsApp the way a phone would. WhatsApp can **permanently ban a number** that behaves like a spam sender, with no appeal. The CRM protects your numbers on purpose: it sends slowly, spaces messages like a human would, and sometimes holds a message back. When it does, it tells you why. Please read the **Safety rules** chapter before your first campaign, and use a number you can afford to lose while you are learning.
:::
