---
title: "Safety rules in plain words"
description: 'The rules that protect your numbers, and why a message is sometimes held.'
---

These protect your numbers. They are not bugs and mostly cannot be switched off.

| Rule | Why it exists |
| --- | --- |
| **Sending is paced** | A human types one message at a time. The CRM spaces messages out, adds small random gaps, slows down at night and "types" before sending. |
| **New numbers start slowly** | A brand-new number that suddenly sends hundreds of messages is the classic ban pattern. The daily allowance grows with time and with replies received. |
| **Daily and hourly limits** | A ceiling per number. Nobody, including admins, can raise it. |
| **Messages vary** | Hundreds of identical messages look like spam. Use `{{name}}` and `{option|option}` in campaigns. |
| **Do-not-contact** | Being reported is the fastest route to a ban. People who asked to stop are never messaged. Only an admin can knowingly override it, per message or per campaign, with a reason. |
| **Mid-conversation and cooldown protection** | Campaigns skip people a colleague is talking to, and people messaged in the last 14 days. |
| **Health scoring** | The CRM watches delivery and replies for each number, slows or quarantines a number that looks risky, and pauses its campaigns if WhatsApp pushes back. |
| **Everything is recorded** | Overrides, assignments, imports, exports and what the AI did are saved in an audit log with who did them. |

:::tip
The best protection is a number that people reply to. Message people who expect to hear from you, and answer replies promptly.
:::
